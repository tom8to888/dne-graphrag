Welcome to Issue 57 of the ITF Coaching & Sport Science
Review. The articles in this issue cover a variety of topics
including female tennis player development, recovery
strategies, momentum during match play, parents and tennis,
the use of slower balls in wheelchair tennis, the double handed
forehand and the impact of fatigue on serve velocity.
The programmes for the five 2012 Regional Coaches
Conferences by BNP Paribas, commencing with the first in
León (Mexico) in September, can all be found on the ITF
coaching weblet as well as information on how to enroll. The
ITF are pleased to announce that the keynote speakers include
Beni Linder (SWZ), Paul Dent (GBR), Mike Barrell (GBR), David
Sanz (ESP), Bernard Pestre (FRA), Gabriel Jaramillo (USA), Ivan
Molina (COL), Louis Cayer (CAN), Mark Kovacs (USA) and Max
de Vylder (GB). In addition to this line up of speakers, both
regional and national experts will also present at the
Conferences.

Coaches who are interested in the Regional biannual Coaches
Conference should approach their National Associations who
will have received detailed information regarding the
conferences.

The European Coaches Symposium will also take place in
Helsinki, Finland from 24th -28th October and coaches
